01 — Research Identity

Teams as living patterns of interaction.

Most team research treats a group as the sum of its members. My work reframes the team as an evolving web of relationships — who trusts, conflicts with, and depends on whom — and traces how those patterns shift moment to moment to shape performance.

Drawing on social-network analysis, dynamic modeling, and multiteam-systems research, I develop theory and methods that capture what static measures miss: the timing, structure, and micro-foundations of how people actually work together.
